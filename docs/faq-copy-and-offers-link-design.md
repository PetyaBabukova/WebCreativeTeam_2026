## Last-row correction from the user

The user reviewed the 13-card version and requested that the final row's three cards align left and keep the same closed width and active expansion as cards in a full five-card row. The previously approved stretch decision is superseded.

On desktop screens only, use an empty `::after` flex spacer for a row containing exactly three cards. Set `content: ""`, `display: block`, `pointer-events: none`, and `flex: 2 1 calc(var(--faq-gap) + var(--faq-card-inset) + var(--faq-card-inset))`. A real card has a zero flex basis plus its padding and border (`--faq-card-inset`); the spacer must account for two missing card insets and their internal gap. With three actual flex gaps, the free space is then `row width − 5·inset − 4·gap`, the same as a full five-card row. The flex grow sum is 5 closed and 6.25 when one card opens, also matching a full row. The first three visible cards align with the first three above, and no placeholder card enters the DOM or accessibility tree. Apply the rule in `@media screen and (width > 68rem)` so it cannot affect mobile, the breakpoint, or print. Target the exact three-card row with `:has(> .faq__card:nth-child(3):last-child)` as a separate rule. The current approved FAQ contains 13 cards; if that count changes, revisit this selector.

Extend browser tests to compare widths and left positions of the first three cards in full and partial rows when closed, then open the first card in each row in turn and compare their measured widths and left positions after animation settles. Check near the desktop breakpoint and at a wide viewport, plus no generated spacer at mobile and exactly 68rem. Check a desktop no-JavaScript view for an extra line gap. No copy, link, or animation timing changes are part of this correction.
